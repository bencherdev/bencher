## Claude Helps Save Christmas

Like many others, over the 2025 winter holiday I had a chance to really use Claude Opus 4.5. I was in London visiting my sister’s family with my wife and child. My sister had recently given birth to twins, so I was supposed to be there to help out. Instead, I was hunkered down upstairs trying to fight off email spam and DDoS attacks against Bencher. Claude was super helpful in diagnosing what was happening and ideating possible solutions.

I ended up migrating Bencher behind Cloudflare and setting up reCAPTCHA. It was still me doing most of the work, but Opus 4.5 was a noticeable step up in its problem solving capabilities across many competing design constraints. Though, it did suggest that I make a change that bricked [the convenience script for installing the Bencher CLI][bencher install cli] for half a day. Oops.
